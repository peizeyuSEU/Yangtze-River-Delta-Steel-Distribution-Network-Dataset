# v0.2 candidate DC location study

This is a non-destructive candidate-location study. v0.1.0-preview deliberately uses common city-centre points for DCs and markets so that the public case is reproducible and does not imply an enterprise warehouse address. That choice creates eight same-city 0 km arcs when a DC and market share a city.

For a future v0.2 candidate, market nodes continue to represent aggregated city demand, while DC nodes use representative logistics parks or facilities. A representative park is not a real enterprise warehouse or customer address. Candidate selection requires a public source, repeatable identification, storage/distribution or intermodal relevance, and a defensible relationship to steel or industrial logistics.

The register separates exact addresses, named logistics POIs, official project locations, and official-boundary representative points. Coordinates are intentionally blank where evidence is insufficient. No city-centre coordinate, random map click, government office, airport terminal, or town-government point is silently substituted for a warehouse candidate.

The current table does not enter the v0.1 build, does not regenerate OSRM distances, and does not create v0.2 processed data. A future v0.2 release would require geocoding caches, distance recomputation, sensitivity comparison, model evaluation, and a new lineage version before it could replace v0.1.
